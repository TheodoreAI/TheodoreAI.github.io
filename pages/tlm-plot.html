How it works

For a transmission line measurement, the total resistance between two adjacent contacts follows

RT = 2Rc + (Rsh/W)·d

where d is the spacing between contacts, W is the contact width, Rsh is the sheet resistance of the semiconductor layer, and Rc is the specific contact resistance at the metal-semiconductor interface. Measuring RT across several contact pairs of different spacing and fitting a line lets you separate the two: the slope gives Rsh/W, and extrapolating back to d = 0 gives the y-intercept, 2Rc.
