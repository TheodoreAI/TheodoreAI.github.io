How the textures are made

Nothing here is a photo or a downloaded asset. Each texture is drawn at runtime with the Canvas 2D API — noise, streaks, and gradients composited into a small tileable image — and then handed to Three.js as a CanvasTexture. Bark is vertical noise streaks on brown; leaves and grass are mottled green noise at different scales; clouds are soft radial gradients rendered onto transparent sprites.
